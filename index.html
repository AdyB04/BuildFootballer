Build-a-Footballer

You get dealt one footballer at a time. Take one attribute from each and fill all 8 slots. Then we simulate your player's whole career.

Choose a position

How it works
  • Each turn you're dealt a random player. Tap one of their stats to lock it into your build.
  • Each slot can only be filled once, so you sometimes have to settle for a weaker stat.
  • You get 2 skips per game to pass on a player.
  • Your position decides which stats matter most for your overall rating.
  • Your career runs from age 18 to retirement: form, injuries, transfers, trophies and Ballon d'Ors.
  • Daily challenge: everyone gets the same players for each position today.
